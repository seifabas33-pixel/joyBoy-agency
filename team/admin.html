Nobody is assigned to a hotel yet. Assign staff from their profile (Roster → open a person → Hotel).

Every shift is judged on its own: checked in by the shift start + grace → Present; later → Late · needs decision; no check-in after that time → Absent. The day is Present when every shift was attended, Half day when some were, Absent when none. Staff who could not check in send you a note per shift; late shifts and notes appear above the table with Present / Excused / Absent buttons. Use Day mark for sick days, vacation, days off or a whole-day correction; it wins over the shifts in the export. In the plan card you choose, per day, where each shift checks in.

Shifts

Staff check in and out for each shift separately. Clear a name to remove that shift. Grace = minutes after the start that still count as on time; later is "Late · needs decision".

Spots — places a shift can check in

Stand at the spot and tap "Use my current location", or paste the pin from Google Maps. 100–200 m is a sensible radius for one area. Spots are stored when you press Save hotel. In the Attendance tab you choose, per day, which spot each shift uses.
