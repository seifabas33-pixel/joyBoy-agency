Base pay: monthly salary minus absent days (salary ÷ days in month) and half of that for half days; a daily rate is paid per day worked (half days count half). Sick, vacation, excused and off days are paid — add a deduction if not. Commission comes from the Sales tab. Mark paid freezes the numbers and publishes the payslip to the person; until then they see nothing. Set salaries in the roster: open a person → Salary.

Items & commission

What staff can sell. Guests pay in any mix of EGP, USD and EUR; you enter each currency when you record the sale. Currency here is the currency of the price and of the commission per unit. Comm./unit is a fixed sum for every ticket or piece sold. The % column is optional (a share of what was collected, per currency) — leave it at 0 if you pay per unit.

Tap a shift letter to switch it on or off for that person and day, or tap off for a day off. Nothing marked means the person works every shift (today's behaviour). Every shift is judged on its own: checked in by the shift start + grace → Present; later → Late · needs decision; no check-in after that time → Absent. The day is Present when every shift was attended, Half day when some were, Absent when none. Staff who could not check in send you a note per shift; late shifts and notes appear above the table with Present / Excused / Absent buttons. Use Day mark for sick days, vacation, days off or a whole-day correction; it wins over the shifts in the export. In the plan card you choose, per day, where each shift checks in.

Add a hotel

Stand where the staff check in (stage, meeting area) and tap: it shows the distance the server will see.

Shifts

Staff check in and out for each shift separately. Clear a name to remove that shift. Grace = minutes after the start that still count as on time; later is "Late · needs decision".

Spots — places a shift can check in

Stand at the spot and tap "Use my current location", or paste the pin from Google Maps. 100–200 m is a sensible radius for one area. Spots are stored when you press Save hotel. In the Attendance tab you choose, per day, which spot each shift uses.
