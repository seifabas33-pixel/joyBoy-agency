1Joy Boy Agency

A managed entertainment department for Red Sea resorts — programme, cast, roster and running order, delivered as one accountable unit instead of a collection of freelancers.

2 Resorts on our books True Beach Resort (current) and Casa Blue Beach Resort, Marsa Alam
268 Staff-days delivered this season July and August 2026 at True Beach, logged person by person
115 Show slots in one Marsa Alam season Casa Blue, May–June — a repertoire, not a repeated night
What we run
  • The full evening programme — dance, oriental, fire, live singers, DJ nights and headline theme nights on a rotating repertoire
  • The daytime line — aqua gym, sports, pool and beach parties, foam party
  • A children's line — staffed daily sessions, nightly Mini Disco, a weekly kids event with costumed characters
  • Live music — instrumentalists and singers on rotation across venues
How we run it
  • A weekly action plan — every event carries its setup, costume-check and soundcheck deadlines and a named owner of the running order
  • A daily attendance register — presence, day-off, vacation and absence per person, totalled monthly
  • A published guest programme — on a board and by QR code, so guests can actually find what you pay to stage
  • Monthly reporting — programme punctuality, sessions delivered, and review mentions against a baseline

The full evidence pack — Anjum's position across TripAdvisor, HolidayCheck and Booking, the seven gaps guests have named, and our operating records — is in the companion dashboard we presented alongside this proposal.
