The agency

A managed entertainment department — one accountable unit, not a collection of freelancers.
What we run
  • The full evening programme — dance, oriental, fire, live singers, DJ nights and headline theme nights on a rotating repertoire
  • The daytime line — aqua gym, sports, pool and beach parties, foam party
  • A children's line — staffed daily sessions, nightly Mini Disco, a weekly kids event with costumed characters
  • Live music — instrumentalists and singers on rotation across venues
How we run it
  • A weekly action plan — every event carries its setup, costume-check and soundcheck deadlines and a named owner of the running order
  • A daily attendance register — presence, day-off, vacation and absence per person, totalled monthly
  • A published guest programme — on a board and by QR code
  • Monthly reporting — punctuality, sessions delivered, review mentions

08

Questions, answered

The things hotel managers ask us first.
How is pricing structured?
Per resort, never from a generic list. After a brief and a walk of the property we propose a programme, a team size and a full entertainment budget for that hotel. Ask on WhatsApp and we will send it.
What is included in every engagement?
A resident team with an accountable team leader; the weekly action plan with named owners and hard setup times; a daily attendance register; a published guest programme with a QR schedule; and a monthly report on punctuality, sessions delivered and guest-review mentions.
How many nights a week does the programme run?
Seven. An evening show every night on a rotating repertoire, the daytime line, the children's line every day and live music on rotation across venues.
Do you run the children's programme as well?
Yes. A dedicated kids club entertainer is part of the team, not borrowed from it: staffed daily sessions, a nightly Mini Disco and a weekly character event.
Where do you operate?
The Egyptian Red Sea: Marsa Alam, Hurghada and Sahl Hasheesh. The team lives at the resort for the season; we are not visiting acts.
Which resorts have you worked with?
Casa Blue Beach Resort in Marsa Alam (2024 season) and True Beach Resort in Marsa Alam (2026 season). The awards and guest reviews on this page come from those two properties.
How do we start?
Message Mr. Moaz on WhatsApp or email joyboyentertainmentagency@gmail.com. We take a brief, visit the property, and come back with a tailored programme and budget.
Can we see the shows before deciding?
Yes — the reels on this page are unedited clips from the floor, and we can arrange a visit to a running programme in season.
