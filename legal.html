Nothing personal. The site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It sets no cookies. If you switch between the dark and light theme, that single preference is stored in your own browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device.

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we use your name and contact details only to reply and to prepare the proposal you asked for. We do not sell or share them. Write to the address above at any time to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Cookie notice
